# Google Play listing — Khotwa AI

## English
**Title:** Khotwa AI: Voice Screen Guide
**Short description (80):** Tell it what you want to do. It guides you on any screen, step by step, by voice.
**Full description:**
Khotwa AI is your smart guide on any screen. Tell it what you want to get done, like applying for a scholarship, filling a government form or changing a phone setting, and it guides you by voice while a small circle points to the exact button to tap.

- Built-in AI that works instantly, no keys and no setup
- Talk naturally and hear the answer
- A small circle shows the next button, step by step
- 9 languages with local voice accents
- On-device AI on supported phones: private and offline
- Never reads passwords, never enters banking apps, never taps for you

Accessibility service use: Khotwa uses the Accessibility API only to read the names of on-screen buttons and draw a guidance circle while you ask for help. It does not read passwords or field contents and performs no actions on your behalf.
